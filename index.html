01 / The experience

What is Gakuran?

The game describes itself as an intended slice-of-life experience — even if players sometimes turn the school into a battleground.

🏫

School-life roleplay

Explore the school and surrounding town, hang out with other students, and make your own stories instead of following a fixed quest line.

🥊

Player combat

Combat is part of the experience too. Learn the basic controls and practice timing before getting into fights with other players.

🎭

Make your character

Shape your student identity and appearance. Use the in-game Stats menu to review the customization options available to you.

03 / New student checklist

Getting started

Start slow, learn the map, and get comfortable with the controls before challenging experienced players.

01

Open the Roblox experience

Visit the game page and check its current access requirements. Community guides report that joining the game's Roblox group may be required.

02

Set up your student

Load in and look through the Stats menu for the character settings and customization options.

03

Explore before fighting

Get familiar with the campus and town. Watch how other players interact and learn where useful places are.

04

Practice combat basics

Community-reported keyboard controls: press T to toggle combat stance, use M1 for a light attack and M2 for a heavy attack. Check in-game settings in case controls change.

Controls and access instructions are based on community-published guides, not an official developer manual. They may change after updates.
04 / Style mastery

Awakening guides

Train an eligible fighting style through three stages to unlock its V2. Requirements below reflect the latest community-tracked quest list.

Before you start: Equip the style you want to awaken. Only progress for your currently equipped style counts, but progress is saved separately for each style. Check Stats → Mastery Stage and the in-game Awakening page. Stage 2 and 3 often have no progress counter, so listen to your character's inner thoughts for hints.
01 / Equip the style

Progress only counts for the fighting style currently equipped. Switching styles does not erase saved progress.

02 / Complete all 3 stages

Finish Stage 1, Stage 2 and the final trial. Completing Stage 3 automatically unlocks that style's V2.

03 / Toggle your V2

Use the Awakening Panel to turn V2 on or off. You cannot change it while in combat or while downed.

How to redeem codes

01

Meet the requirements

Community sources report that you may need to join the Gakuran Roblox group, have at least 5 Roblox friends, and reach 1 hour of playtime. Requirements may vary after updates.

02

Open the game menu

Join Gakuran, then click the drawer/menu icon on the left side of the screen.

03

Choose Codes

Open the Codes tab in the menu and paste the exact code. Capitalization matters.

04

Redeem and check

Press Redeem. Look for the game's success or error message to confirm whether it worked on your account.

If a code marked active fails, check spelling, playtime and group requirements, make sure you have not already redeemed it, and rejoin an updated server. This page is a manually maintained list; it cannot test codes against your account automatically.

07 / Quick answers

FAQ

Is Gakuran a fighting game or a roleplay game?

Both elements are present. The official description calls it a slice-of-life experience, while Roblox categorizes it under Action and Battlegrounds & Fighting.

What year is Gakuran set in?

The Roblox game description says May 2007 in Japan.

Why can't I join the game?

Check the Roblox game page for current access requirements. Some community guides say players may need to join the creator's Roblox group first.

Are these controls guaranteed to be current?

No. They are community-reported keyboard controls. Updates, device type, or settings may change how you play.
